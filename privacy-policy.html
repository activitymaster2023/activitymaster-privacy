1. Introduction

Activity Master ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Activity Master mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App. By using Activity Master, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or a region with applicable privacy laws, we will request your consent before displaying personalized advertisements, as described in Section 5.

2. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

Activity Master does not require account registration. All activity data, tasks, sessions, and settings you create are stored locally on your device using SQLite and are never transmitted to our servers.

b. Automatically Collected Information

When you use Activity Master, our third-party advertising partner, Google AdMob, may automatically collect certain information to serve advertisements. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • General location (country or region, derived from IP address)
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads

We do not directly collect or have access to this information.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above solely to:

  • Display advertisements within Activity Master through Google AdMob
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

All personal activity data (activities, tasks, sessions, scores) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal data. We implement reasonable technical measures within the App, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data on your device.

5. Advertising and Your Choices

Activity Master uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on your location and consent choice:

  • Personalized Ads: If you consent, AdMob may use your advertising ID and usage data to show ads tailored to your interests.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: If you do not consent or opt out, AdMob will show contextual ads that are not based on your personal profile.
You can review or change your ad preferences at any time through Settings → Ad Preferences within the App.

For more information on how Google collects and uses data for advertising, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

To opt out of interest-based advertising on your Android device, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal information held about you
  • Withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time (via Settings → Ad Preferences)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (EEA users)

Since Activity Master stores all user-generated data locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing app data through your device settings.
